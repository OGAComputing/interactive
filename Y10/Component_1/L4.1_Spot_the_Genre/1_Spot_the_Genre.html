Spot the Genre

You can name a film's genre from a few seconds of its trailer. That's because every genre has things we expect to see, and producers put them there on purpose.

Warm-up · optional
✓

Which genre?

GenreA type of product. Horror, science fiction and sitcom are all genres.
IconographyObjects, costumes, settings: the things you see that tell you the genre.
HorrorMade to scare you: danger, the dark, monsters.
Science fictionScience and the future: space, aliens, robots, new technology.
SuperheroHeroes with special powers who save people.
FantasyMagic and made-up worlds: dragons, wizards, spells.

🎯 Each card is a piece of iconography. Which genre does it tell you? Two cards in each box.

Done? Now try Task 1: three trailers.

1

Three trailers

🎯 Watch the clip from each trailer. Then, for each one, name its genre and the iconography in it that tells you. Answer all six questions, then check.

💡 What to look for

Look at what's on screen, not the story: the objects (a weapon, a vehicle, a creature), the costumes (a cape, a space suit) and the settings (a desert planet, a city at night, a Viking village).

Every iconography option could be in a film of that genre. Only one is in this clip, so watch it.

2

Find the iconography

🎯 A film still and a series poster. Pick a numbered pin, then click the part of the picture it asks for. Place all seven pins, then check.

💡 How to decide

Iconography is something you can see and point at: a character, a costume, a prop (an object they use) or a setting (a place).

Ask: "If I saw only this part, would I still guess the genre?" If yes, it's iconography.

3

Name it from its conventions

Codes and conventionsWhat we expect: the things a genre usually has. Every sector has them: a soap opera, a newspaper, a website and a game all have their own.

🎯 Six real products, hidden. Each clue is one convention of its genre, and a new clue appears every few seconds (watch the bar). Name the genre as early as you dare: guess on clue 1 for ★★★. A wrong guess loses gold and makes you wait longer than the next clue takes, so read before you guess.

💡 How to decide

Ask: "Where would I meet this?" On TV, on paper, on a screen I click, or in a game I control?

Then: "What does it always have?" A cliffhanger, a laugh, a cash prize, a masthead, a navigation bar, a lap counter?

4

Describe a genre

🎯 Pick one of the three trailers from Task 1. Explain its genre, its iconography and the conventions it follows. Full sentences: your teacher reads these.

💡 An example, for a different film: Five Nights at Freddy's

Genre: Its genre is horror. I can tell straight away because the poster is dark and a huge robot bear is staring at us.

Iconography: The iconography includes the animatronic bear and the empty pizza restaurant at night, which shows the genre because something friendly has been made scary.

Conventions: One convention of horror films is a hero trapped somewhere at night, and Mike is stuck on the night shift with no way out.
