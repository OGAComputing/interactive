💡 Stuck on the era? Clues to look for

Before 2000: black-and-white photos, big blocky pixels, painted posters, old prices (like 52p), arcade machines and Game Boys.

2000 onwards: sharp HD pictures, phones and apps, YouTube and social media, 3D game worlds.
