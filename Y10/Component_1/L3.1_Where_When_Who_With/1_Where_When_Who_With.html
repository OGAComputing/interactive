Where, When, Who With

The same product can feel completely different depending on where you are, when it is and who you're with, and on how much you take part.

WhereThe place you use the product: at home, on the bus, in a stadium.
WhenThe time you use it: live as it's shown, the next day, on a special day.
Who withOn your own, with family or friends, or in a big crowd.

3

Which kind of involvement?

Interactive featuresParts of a product you click, tap or choose that respond to you.
Online votingThe audience votes on a website or app, and the votes change what happens.
Consumer-generated contentContent the audience makes themselves and shares through the product.

🎯 Each card is a way the audience gets involved. Sort them: three cards in each box.

💡 How to tell them apart

Did the audience make something other people can see? → consumer-generated content.

Did they vote on something, and the result changes what happens? → online voting.

Did they press, tap or choose something, and the product answered just them? → interactive features.

4

Passive or active?

Passive audienceTakes the product in without doing anything back: it's on in the background, or they just sit and watch.
Active audienceTakes part: chooses, talks about it, votes, chats or makes something with it.

🎯 Place each card on the line from passive to active. Clear cases go near an end; some sit in between. Then explain the two ends.

💡 What to look for

Is the person doing anything back: choosing, talking, typing, voting, making?

Does the situation make a difference? Half asleep after a big dinner is more passive than shouting guesses with your family.

5

  1. Open Task 2 and read your diary to a partner. Listen to theirs.
  2. Pick one product from your diary and one from theirs that were used in very different situations.
  3. For each one, write its situation and how involved the audience was. Then write the biggest difference.
💡 An example, for Mario Kart World

Situation: I played Mario Kart World on the bus on my own, so the situation meant I only played short races between stops.

Involvement: I was an active audience because I chose my kart and track, which are interactive features.
