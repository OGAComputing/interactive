Apply it: The Great British Bake Off

🎯 Write about a product you haven't studied in class, using the terms from Learning Aim A. About 15 minutes. Use the evidence file, and what you know about the show.

Evidence file

  • 1Amateur bakers compete in a tent over ten weeks. One leaves each week, and the winner gets a cake stand and flowers, not money.
  • 2It began on BBC Two in 2010. Since 2017 it has been on Channel 4, which is paid for by adverts, not the licence fee.
  • 3It is made by Love Productions, and shown on Tuesday evenings from September to November. Every episode is also on Channel 4's streaming service.
  • 4It is one of Channel 4's most-watched shows every year, watched by families, students and older viewers.
  • 5While it is on, fans post photos of their own bakes and jokes about "soggy bottoms" on social media.
  • 6There are spin-offs: Junior Bake Off for young bakers, and An Extra Slice, a comedy chat show. Recipes from every challenge are on the show's website.

No sentence starters this time, as in the real assessment. Each answer must use a word from each box under its question. Full sentences: your teacher reads these.
