How Grading Works

A guide for teachers: assigning activities and collecting grades in Google Classroom

← Back to Activities Hub
Before you begin
PRE

What you need

  • Your school Google account
  • At least one active class in Google Classroom
  • A desktop browser (Chrome recommended)
No setup There is nothing to install. Sign in on the hub and you can assign activities and collect grades straight away, from any school.
How it works
1

Sign in on the Activities Hub

On the CS Activities Hub, click Sign in to Assign to Classroom in the Teacher Tools bar and allow every permission Google asks for.

A class selector appears, and every activity card gets an Assign button.

2

Assign an activity

  1. Choose the class in the class selector (and a Classroom topic, if you want one)
  2. Find the activity (use the Year tabs or the search box)
  3. Click Assign to … on its card
Use the Assign button Grades can only be written to assignments the hub created. If you paste an activity link into Classroom yourself, students' work is still attached, but no grade appears.
3

Students complete the activity

Students open the assignment from Classroom and sign in with their school account. As they work, the page saves a copy of their answers and their current score to their submission. They see Score saved for your teacher ✅.

4

Collect the grades

Open the hub and sign in. It finds every score students have saved since you last signed in, across all your classes, and writes each one into Classroom as a draft grade. This usually takes a few seconds. Wait for ✅ Grades up to date in the Teacher Tools bar.

  • With no class chosen, ⟳ Sync grades rechecks every score saved in the last 90 days.
  • With a class chosen, it checks every assignment in that class, however old. This is slower, but it catches work saved before this way of grading began.
  • Grades only go up. If a student redoes an activity and scores lower, they keep their best score.
5

Return grades to students

  1. Open the assignment in Google Classroom and go to Student Work
  2. Review the draft grades. You can change any score
  3. Select the students and click Return
Troubleshooting
Problem Fix
A grade hasn't appeared in Classroom Open the hub, choose the class and click ⟳ Sync grades. Check the assignment was made with the hub's Assign button, not a pasted link.
A student sees "Score not saved — tell your teacher" Usually a skipped permission box: they should click Sign in again and tick every box. If they have already handed the work in, they need to Unsubmit it, then do one more task so the page can save again.
Activity cards show no Assign button Sign in first: click Sign in to Assign to Classroom in the Teacher Tools bar on the hub.
Class selector is empty after signing in Check your Classroom classes are Active, not archived. Archived classes are not shown.
