Knowledge — the three ways to interpret the Bible
Interpretation What they believe
Fundamentalist (Literal) The Bible is the direct word of God and is completely true. It must be understood exactly as written — not as a symbol or metaphor.
Conservative The Bible is inspired by God. Most of it is historically true, but some parts may use symbolic or poetic language. Scientific ideas may be accepted alongside the Bible.
Liberal The Bible is a collection of human writings about God. Many stories are understood as metaphors or symbols. The spiritual message matters more than literal facts.

Understand what Fundamental Christians specifically believe about the creation of the world.
Knowledge — Fundamentalist vs Liberal views on Genesis 1

Fundamentalist view: Genesis 1 is a literally true and historically accurate account. God created everything in six actual 24-hour days. Evolution is rejected because the Bible says God created everything directly. The Bible is the direct word of God and must be understood exactly as written.

Liberal view: Genesis 1 is symbolic or poetic — it teaches that God is the creator, but is not meant to be a science textbook. Scientific theories like evolution can be accepted alongside faith. The spiritual message matters more than literal facts.
