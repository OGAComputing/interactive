📚Key Vocabulary

Prosperity Gospel

A Christian teaching that God rewards faith, prayer, and generosity with material success, wealth, and good health.

Blessing

God's favour shown as a gift — this could be wisdom, wealth, a large family, long life, or success in work.

Parable

A short story told by Jesus to share a deeper moral or spiritual lesson about God and how people should live.

Controversial

A topic or idea that causes strong disagreement between people — different sides hold different views.

Seed Faith

An idea associated with Kenneth Copeland: giving money to the church is like planting a seed that God will multiply back to the believer.

Joel Osteen

A US pastor who leads one of the largest churches in America. He teaches that God wants believers to live full, positive, and successful lives.

Kenneth Copeland

A US preacher who teaches that faith, prayer, and financial giving to the church leads to God's material blessings — the idea of Seed Faith.

Stewardship

The responsible and generous use of God's gifts — including money and possessions — to help others and support His work.

In the New Testament, Jesus warned that wealth can be a spiritual barrier and told the Parable of the Rich Man and Lazarus, where the rich man who ignored the poor was punished after death.

Read each argument and decide whether it supports or challenges the Prosperity Gospel.
Knowledge

Arguments FOR the Prosperity Gospel: The Old Testament shows God blessing faithful figures with material wealth. Wealth can be used to help others and support God's work. Preachers like Osteen encourage faith and hope.

Arguments AGAINST the Prosperity Gospel: Jesus warned about the dangers of wealth. It may pressure poor believers to give money they cannot afford. Jesus himself lived simply. The Parable of Lazarus shows wealth does not guarantee God's favour.

The Old Testament shows that Abraham, Solomon, and Job all received great wealth because of their faithfulness — so God may still reward faith with material blessings today.

Jesus warned that it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom — suggesting that wealth can be a spiritual barrier.

Wealth and success gained through faith can be used to support God's work and to help those who are in need in the wider community.

The Prosperity Gospel may put pressure on poor believers to donate money they cannot afford, promising blessings that may never come to them.

Preachers like Joel Osteen encourage people to have faith, hope, and a positive outlook on life — which can bring genuine comfort to those who are struggling.

Jesus chose to live a simple life, helped the poor, and warned against greed — the Prosperity Gospel's focus on material gain contradicts this example.

Solomon asked God for wisdom rather than riches, and received both — suggesting that seeking the right things can lead God to bless a person with prosperity as well.

The Parable of Lazarus shows that wealth in this life does not guarantee God's favour — the rich man who ignored the poor was punished after death.
