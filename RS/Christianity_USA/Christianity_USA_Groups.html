Religious Studies - Christianity in the USA

What are the largest Christian groups in the USA?

Christianity in the USA is diverse. These tasks explore how different denominations share core Christian beliefs while differing in authority, worship, lifestyle and baptism.

Catholic Baptist Methodist Evangelical Protestant Latter-day Saints Amish
Big idea Christians may share belief in Jesus and use of the Bible, but denominations can differ in sources of authority, forms of worship, baptism and everyday religious life.
Authority Bible, tradition, leaders and community can guide belief.
Worship Services may be formal, informal, home-based or temple-centred.
Baptism Groups differ over infant baptism and believer's baptism.
Lifestyle Faith can shape family, charity, mission and community life.

iInfo hunt knowledge

Catholic

Jesus is the Son of God. The Bible and Church Tradition guide belief. Worship is structured and the Eucharist is central. Catholics emphasise sacraments, charity and family.

Evangelical Protestant

Personal relationship with Jesus, faith alone and being born again are important. The Bible is the highest authority. Worship is informal, expressive and focused on preaching.

Methodist

God's grace is received through faith and should be shown through good works. Methodists use the Bible, reason, tradition and experience, and often focus on social justice.

Baptist

Baptists teach personal faith in Jesus, Bible alone, local church independence and believer's baptism by full immersion.

Latter-day Saints

LDS Christians use the Bible, Book of Mormon and modern prophets. They focus on family life, missionary work, temple rituals and a health code.

Amish

Amish Christians value adult commitment, humility, simple living, home-based worship, plain dress and avoiding many forms of modern technology.
