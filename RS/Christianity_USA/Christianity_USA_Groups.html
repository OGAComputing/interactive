Knowledge

Catholic is the largest single Christian denomination. Baptist, Methodist and Evangelical Protestant are Protestant traditions. Latter-day Saints and Amish are later Christian groups with distinctive histories and community practices.

⛪Denomination profiles

Catholic

Structured Mass, sacraments, the Pope, Church Tradition and the Eucharist are central.

Baptist

Bible alone, independent local churches and believer's baptism by full immersion.

Latter-day Saints

Bible, Book of Mormon, modern prophets, missionary work and strong family focus.

Amish

Plain living, simple home worship, community tradition and separation from modern society.

Methodist

Faith shown through good works, social action, justice and community service.

Evangelical Protestant

Personal relationship with Jesus, being born again, Bible authority and sharing faith.

🔍Info hunt knowledge

Catholic

Jesus is the Son of God. The Bible and Church Tradition guide belief. Worship is structured and the Eucharist is central. Catholics emphasise sacraments, charity and family.

Evangelical Protestant

Personal relationship with Jesus, faith alone and being born again are important. The Bible is the highest authority. Worship is informal, expressive and focused on preaching.

Methodist

God's grace is received through faith and should be shown through good works. Methodists use the Bible, reason, tradition and experience, and often focus on social justice.

Baptist

Baptists teach personal faith in Jesus, Bible alone, local church independence and believer's baptism by full immersion.

Latter-day Saints

LDS Christians use the Bible, Book of Mormon and modern prophets. They focus on family life, missionary work, temple rituals and a health code.

Amish

Amish Christians value adult commitment, humility, simple living, home-based worship, plain dress and avoiding many forms of modern technology.

Knowledge

Catholic baptism is usually for infants, with water poured on the head to welcome them into the Church. Baptist believer's baptism is for people old enough to choose faith and is usually by full immersion.
