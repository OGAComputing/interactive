Joseph Smith

Founded the LDS Church in 1830. He claimed God and Jesus appeared to him in a vision and that he translated a new book of scripture from golden plates.

First Vision

Joseph Smith's account of praying for guidance and being visited by both God and Jesus, who told him not to join any existing church.

Book of Mormon

A sacred LDS scripture which Joseph Smith said he translated from golden plates buried in a hillside, believed to restore the original Christian faith.

Living Prophet

An LDS Church leader believed to receive direct guidance from God for the whole Church today, just as prophets did in biblical times.

Baptism for the Dead

A ritual in which a living LDS member is baptised on behalf of a deceased ancestor, offering that person a chance to accept the faith in the afterlife.

Word of Wisdom

The LDS health code, which teaches members to avoid alcohol, tobacco, coffee, and tea for physical and spiritual wellbeing.

Persecution

Being treated unfairly or harshly because of your beliefs — early LDS members faced hostility and were forced to move many times.

Martyrdom

Dying for your beliefs or faith. Joseph Smith's death at the hands of a mob in 1844 is regarded by LDS members as martyrdom.

Recall the key events in the founding and early history of the LDS Church.
Knowledge

The LDS Church was founded in the 1830s by Joseph Smith, who said that God and Jesus appeared to him and told him not to join any existing church. In 1827 he said he found golden plates buried in a hill and translated them into the Book of Mormon. Joseph Smith was jailed and killed by a mob in 1844. Brigham Young then led the community westward to Utah.

Sort distinctive LDS beliefs and practices into the correct categories.
Knowledge

Scripture & Authority: LDS members follow the Bible, the Book of Mormon, and the Doctrine and Covenants. A Living Prophet guides the whole Church with ongoing revelation from God.

Worship & Ceremony: Temples are used for special rituals including temple marriage, baptism for the dead, and other ceremonies. Members meet in local meetinghouses on Sundays. Family Home Evening is a weekly time for family prayer and discussion.

Daily Life: The Word of Wisdom is a health code requiring members to avoid alcohol, tobacco, coffee and tea. Young adults are encouraged to serve as missionaries. The family is seen as central to God's plan.

Compare how the LDS Church and the Catholic Church differ on key practices.
Knowledge

LDS: Baptism from age 8; scripture includes the Bible, Book of Mormon, and Doctrine and Covenants; led by a Living Prophet; unique practices include temple ceremonies and baptism for the dead.

Catholic: Infant baptism (water poured on the head); guided by the Bible and Church Tradition; led by the Pope; the Eucharist (Mass) is central to worship.
