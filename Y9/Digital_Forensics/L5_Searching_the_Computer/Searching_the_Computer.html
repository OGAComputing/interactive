⭐ Finished early? Why SSDs are different

Many new laptops use SSDs (solid state drives, Year 9 Hardware) instead of spinning hard drives. SSDs use a command called TRIM that tells the drive to properly erase deleted blocks in the background, so recovery is much harder.

Question: if Ashley's PC had an SSD with TRIM switched on, would Station 3 have found the photos? Where else might investigators look? (Hint: think about the phone that took them, and cloud backups.)

Station 4

The Unpatched Phone

Ashley's Samsung phone took the photos, but it is locked with a PIN. The lab noticed something: Ashley never installed his updates. Can that get them inside? And what does it mean for your phone?

🩹 What is a security patch? Read

All software has mistakes in it. Some mistakes are security holes (also called vulnerabilities) that let someone do something they shouldn't, like read your messages or get past your lock screen. When the maker finds a hole, they write a fix called a patch and send it out in an update.

Every phone records its security patch level: the date of the newest patch it has installed. Any hole fixed after that date is still open on that phone.

🛡 Protect yourself Switch on automatic updates for your phone, laptop, console and apps. When a device asks you to restart to install updates, do it soon: many fixes only go in after a restart. ⭐ Finished early? Check your own devices
