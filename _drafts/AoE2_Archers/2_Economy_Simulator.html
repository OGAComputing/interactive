Economy Simulator: Archers into Castle

Get from the Dark Age to the Castle Age with an archer army that keeps raiders off your villagers. Your economy has to keep growing the whole time. The clock runs fast, and there will be raids.

How it works

  • You start with 3 villagers on sheep, 200 food, 200 wood and 100 gold. The Town Centre (TC) is doing nothing. Fix that.
  • Queue villagers Q, build houses H, farms F, train archers A. Space pauses the game.
  • Natural food (sheep, boar, berries) runs out. After that, every food villager needs a farm, or they stand around.
  • Aging up and researching at the TC stops villager production while it runs, like in the real game.
  • Raids arrive at set times. Your archers (+25% with Fletching) have to match the raid's strength, or you lose villagers. Loom stops the early scout raid on its own.
  • Reach the Castle Age before 27:00. The game is scored on the Five Idle Sins.
